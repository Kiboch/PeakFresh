About Peak Fresh

Freshness in Every Sip.

At Peak Fresh, we believe a refreshing smoothie should be simple, delicious, and made with care. We prepare fruit smoothies with carefully selected ingredients to bring you great taste in every sip.

From our creamy Banana Bliss to our tropical Pineapple Paradise and refreshing Banana + Watermelon Smoothie, there is a flavour for every mood.

Whether you need a refreshing break during your day or a fruity treat to enjoy on the go, Peak Fresh makes it easy to enjoy a freshly prepared smoothie with convenient pickup.

Ordering is available only in the regions Peak Fresh has enabled. Before checkout, customers are asked to share their device location so we can check availability. Orders are still collected from the store; delivery is not offered through this location check.
